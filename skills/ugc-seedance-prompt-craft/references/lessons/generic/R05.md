id: R05
title: Hands-only shots need a visible arm or a surface
status: active
when: hands_only, unboxing
product: any
lint: hands_support
lint_level: warning
source: harvest 6 Nov 2026: 6 scenes in 4 projects
---
When only hands are in the shot, say what connects them to the world: "her forearms enter from the bottom edge of the frame" or "the box rests on the wooden table and her hands open it". Never leave an object held by hands that come from nowhere.

Why: objects held by disembodied hands were read as floating and failed the physics check; some of these failures may be the checker being too strict on hands-only shots (calibration set).
