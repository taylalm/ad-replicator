Show one name at a time, close and sharp, "exactly as in @ImageN". Keep two-name items for a shot where only one name is readable or where the image reference carries both. Never add note cards, letters, labels or screens with text of their own: the model invents letters.

Why: two names on one sign and handwriting on a note card came out garbled; the smallest video models are weakest here.
