For anything with words or a one-way design, say "the text reads left to right, upright, facing the camera; never mirrored or upside down". Avoid flat-lay shots of such items unless the camera looks from the reading side; prefer the item held up or worn facing the lens.

Why: socks laid flat and an address sign came out mirrored or upside down.
