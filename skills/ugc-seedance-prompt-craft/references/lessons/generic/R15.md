An item nobody wears or holds is described as empty and collapsed: "the stocking hangs flat and empty, the fabric limp, no foot shape inside", "the socks lie flat on the duvet, cuffs collapsed". Never "soles up" or "standing" for empty items, which the model fills with invisible feet. If an empty prop keeps failing, swap it for a prop without a body shape (a folded throw, a box).

Why: empty stockings and socks come out as feet or bodies; the checker fails them as anatomy or cast-count errors.
