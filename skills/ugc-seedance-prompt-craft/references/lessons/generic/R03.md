For a printed or thin product say the material and thickness and forbid volume: "a flat printed graphic on the knit fabric, no raised or sculpted parts", "a 2 mm metal sheet, flat, cut around the outline, no depth". Never write "3D", "sculpted", "plush" or "embossed" unless the product itself is three-dimensional; a product title with "3D" in it describes the artwork style, not the object.

Why: printed sock art came out as clay figures stuck on the sock, flat signs as thick slabs and a felt board as a plush toy.
