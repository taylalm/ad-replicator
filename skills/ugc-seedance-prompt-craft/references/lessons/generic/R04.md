Give each beat its own time range and one action: "0-3 s: only the dog is in the shot, sniffing the pillow; at 3 s the man enters from the right edge and stops behind it; 3-6 s: the dog freezes, the man laughs". Say who is in the first frame and who is not yet. Put the payoff in the second half of the clip, never in the last second.

Why: people appeared before their entrance, a dog kept walking instead of freezing, reveals played backwards and payoffs never happened when the order was left to the model.
