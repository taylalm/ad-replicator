The person image decides the clothes: make or pick a reference wearing exactly the outfit, shoes and hair of the scene, and say in the prompt "the same clothes and shoes as @Image1 in every second". Do not describe different clothes in words; the picture wins over the text.

Why: clothes and shoes drifted from the reference whenever the prompt asked for something the picture did not show.
