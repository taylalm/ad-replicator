Every product shot states the real size from `product.size_statement` with a cue a camera understands: "50 x 60 inches: held up, taller than the child and wider than her arm span", "a 12-inch sign, as wide as his two hands side by side".

Why: video models have no sense of scale; a blanket came out towel-sized and a sign as big as a door. craft.py already requires the dimensions in product shots.
