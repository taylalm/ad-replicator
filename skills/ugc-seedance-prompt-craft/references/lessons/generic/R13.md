---
id: R13
title: Frame feet and legs shots on purpose: face fully in or fully out
status: active
when: feet
product: wearable, flat_print
lint: feet_face
lint_level: warning
source: harvest 6 Nov 2026: 2 scenes in 1 project
---
In a shot of legs, feet or socks either frame below the shoulders on purpose ("framed from the waist down; no face in the shot") or wide enough for the whole head. Never let the top edge cut through a face.

Why: faces cut by the top edge in feet shots failed the composition check twice.
