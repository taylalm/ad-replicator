title: Say how the product is used, mounted, worn or lit
status: active

---
Write ONE `product.use` line from the product page photos: how the item is mounted, worn, carried or lit, which side shows, and what never shows ("screwed flat to the outside of the door with the printed face out; never hung on a rope or string", "carried on the forearm by its handles, the printed side facing out; the plain inner side never shows", "the design is printed on the shade and stays dark; only the bulb glows behind it"). Paste it word for word into every shot that shows the product.

Why: signs on ropes, signs indoors, totes held by the handles, light glowing through a print, hoods with face openings and inner linings showing were the most common product failures, and users asked for these fixes most.
