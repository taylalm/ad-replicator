---
id: R02
title: Keep subjects at a constant distance from the camera
status: active
when: walking, hands_only, unboxing, product
product: any
lint: toward_camera
lint_level: error
source: harvest 6 Nov 2026: 13 scenes in 11 projects; cost test 9 Nov: a board lifted to the lens and a thrown ball changed size (4 retakes)
---
Movement goes across the frame, not toward or away from the lens: "she walks from the left edge to the right edge at the same distance; the camera stays fixed". If someone must walk toward the viewer, "the camera walks backward at the same pace, so she stays the same size". Lift the product to chest height, never up to the lens, and say so ("lifts the board to chest height, no closer to the camera"). Thrown or carried props keep their size ("the ball keeps the same size in flight").

Why: a product lifted to the camera jumps in size between frames, people walking toward a tracking camera come out walking backwards, and feet slide; each was a paid retake.
